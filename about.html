Our Story

Illustration Palette

A creative studio where illustration, branding, and wedding design come together to create visuals that feel intentional, emotional, and timeless.

The Founder & Creative Head

Gracie Verma

Illustration Palette is founded and creatively led by Sneha, also known as Gracie Verma. She is an illustrator and designer with over 6 years of experience across wedding design, branding, fashion illustration, social media management, and event-focused visuals.

With hands-on experience working with international clients and event planning teams, she brings a strong understanding of design aesthetics, cultural nuances, and client expectations. Her work is known for merging tradition with modern design language, resulting in visuals that feel both personal and refined.

As Founder and Creative Head, she personally oversees the creative direction of every project, ensuring consistency, quality, and emotional depth across all designs.

Illustration Palette began in 2020 as a focused illustration practice and gradually expanded into a multidisciplinary design studio. In the early days, the work revolved around custom illustrations and creative visuals. As projects grew more diverse, the studio naturally stepped into branding, wedding stationery, and event-focused design.

Over the years, Illustration Palette has worked on a wide range of projects, including wedding invitation suites, wardrobe planners, custom illustrations for couples, brand identities, social media design, editorial layouts, and event design assets. The studio has contributed to intimate weddings, large-scale multicultural celebrations, and destination weddings across India and international locations.

Working with clients from India, the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom has fostered a deep understanding of cross-cultural aesthetics, traditions, and contemporary design sensibilities. Each project added a new layer of experience, strengthening the studio's ability to design with sensitivity, clarity, and intention.

Our Craft

What We Specialize In

Illustration Palette specializes in custom illustration-driven design. This includes wedding stationery and paper goods, wardrobe planners, signage and layout design, brand identity design, fashion and editorial illustrations, and digital creatives.

Every design is created from scratch and tailored specifically to the client. There are no templates and no repeated styles. Each project reflects the story, culture, and personality of the people behind it.
